01 — Seed

Every painting is a genome.

A painting is just a list of shapes. Each shape is one gene: a form and colour, a place, a size, an angle.

02 — Select

You are the fitness function.

No score, no model of beauty. The paintings you keep are the only ones allowed to breed.

03 — Breed

Recombine. Mutate.

A child takes one side of each parent, then some genes drift. Drag σ and watch the child change.

04 — Repeat

Taste compounds.

Every generation descends only from what you kept, so your eye steers the whole line.
